# Housing Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax.

**Goal:** Rent and purchase prices per municipality for es and fr, on a Housing map and as gems criteria.
**Architecture:**
- `pipeline/compute_housing.py --country es|fr`:
  - readers per source;
  - `pool_nearby` for missing values;
  - `build_output`;
  - `housing_csv_rows`.
- **Web:** `web/src/housing.ts`, plus a `housing` mode in main.ts, plus gems criteria in gems.ts.

**Tech Stack:** Python (openpyxl, xlrd: new), TypeScript/MapLibre, vitest.
**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-22-housing-design.md`

## Global Constraints
- **Paths:** outputs in `web/public/data/<cc>/housing.{json,csv,csvt}`; downloads in `.cache/<cc>/housing/`.
- **JSON row:** `code -> [rent_house, rent_flat, buy_house, buy_flat, s_rh, s_rf, s_bh, s_bf, n_rh, n_rf, n_bh, n_bf, population]`. Values are €/m² rounded to 0.1 for rent and to 10 for buy, `null` when missing. Scope: 0 own, 1 nearby, 2 province, 3 area model.
- **Pooling:** radius 10 km; at least 10 leases (rent) or 5 sales (buy) pooled; count-weighted mean of medians.
- **Gems defaults:** rent on, houses, the default threshold set from the data's rural median (decided in Task 1); buy off.

## Review Focus
1. **Scope labels:** nothing that isn't the municipality's own figure may look like it is (hatch, tooltip, CSV).
2. **Spanish name matching for towns over 25,000:** every row must match exactly one municipality in its province; unmatched rows are reported. Tested in Task 1.
3. **Missing data** (Basque Country/Navarre rent, Alsace-Moselle buy) must be missing, not 0. Tested in Task 1.
4. **Gems hash:** backwards compatible, so old links without rent/buy get the defaults. Tested in Task 2.
5. **Mode switch** with 6 buttons on narrow screens. Checked in the browser.
